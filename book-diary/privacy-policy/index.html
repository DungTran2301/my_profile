Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Welcome to VQuote! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the VQuote mobile application.

Privacy-First Approach: VQuote is designed to work offline. By default, your reading logs, books, quotes, and reading statistics are stored directly on your own device.

1. Information We Collect

Because Book Diary focuses on offline data storage, the types of information we interact with include:

  • Library and Bookshelf Data: Details of the books you catalog, progress tracking records, reading durations, and shelves you create.
  • Reading Diaries and Key Insights: The text highlights, book notes, ideas, and quote logs that you manually input into your personal scrapbook.
  • Application Settings: Your language preferences (English/Vietnamese), theme choices (Dark/Light mode), and persona selections.

2. How Your Data is Stored

All library statistics, notes, and records are written into a private, local SQLite database on your device. We do not have access to this data, nor do we transfer it to external servers, except when you explicitly choose to perform a manual backup or trigger cloud sync services (such as Google Drive or iCloud) as provided in future app updates.

3. Device Permissions

To deliver a premium tracking experience, Book Diary may request access to:

  • Photo Gallery / Camera: Required only when you choose to personalize a book entry by taking a photo of a physical book cover or picking an existing cover image from your gallery.
  • Storage: Necessary to store localized cover photo copies in the app's secure internal directory.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use standard platform services provided by Apple (App Store / iCloud) or Google (Google Play Console / Firebase) to monitor application stability, analyze anonymous crash logs, and deliver critical updates. These services handle metadata in accordance with their respective privacy standards.

5. Your Choices & Data Rights

Since your diary stays on your device, you are in complete control:

  • You can modify, update, or permanently delete any book logs or insights directly from the app interface.
  • Uninstalling the VQuote app will permanently erase the local SQLite database. We highly recommend exporting backups before doing so.
